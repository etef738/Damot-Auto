    Collision repair

    After the crash, the comeback

    From a scraped bumper to a front end that met a pole, Damot takes your car through the same four stages every time. Mechanical damage behind the panels gets fixed in the same building, so nothing is left for another shop.

    Inspect
    Stage 1

    Damage assessment

    We strip back the damaged area and map every point of impact, including the hidden damage behind bumpers and panels, then write the estimate for you or your insurer.

    Structure
    Stage 2

    Frame & body repair

    Bent structure is pulled back to the manufacturer's measurements. Panels are repaired where they can be and replaced where they can't.

    Refinish
    Stage 3

    Paint & refinishing

    Body prep, primer, then colour matched to your car's paint code and blended into the panels next to it, finished with clear coat.

    Deliver
    Stage 4

    Quality check & delivery

    Lights, sensors, alignment and fit are checked, the car is cleaned and road-tested, and we call you to pick it up.

    Just had an accident?

    1. Stay safe. Move out of traffic if you can, turn on your hazards and call 911 if anyone is hurt.
    2. Swap information. Names, phone numbers, plates, licences and insurance details of every driver involved.
    3. Take photos. All vehicles, the damage, the road and any witnesses' contact details.
    4. Report it. In Toronto, a collision with no injuries and more than $5,000 in total damage is reported at a Collision Reporting Centre within 24 hours.
    5. Call us. Ask the tow operator to bring the car to Damot at 1861 O'Connor Drive, then call your insurer.
